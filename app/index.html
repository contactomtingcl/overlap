How much to trust these numbers

Joining two sensors is never exact. We measured the error on years the model never saw (2020–2026) and against 75,718 CONAF fire records (2002–2020).

How to read this

No detection is not the same as no fire. Clouds, smoke and satellite gaps hide fires. Grey hatched cells mean the record could not be built for that month, not zero fire.

Work in progress (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026). Harmonization v1: quantile mapping (validated) in Maule, Ñuble, Biobío and La Araucanía; simple ratios in other regions (not yet validated).
